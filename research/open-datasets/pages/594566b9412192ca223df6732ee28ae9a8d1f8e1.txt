 Hockey Summary Project 
We started out back on January 10, 2001, with the idea to compile hockey summaries in a database format. Our slogan was simple. "By researchers. For researchers." Our cooperative spirit remains true to this day.
The database is still a work in progress but what we are able to present at this point is an ever increasing collection of vintage NHL game summaries. Hopefully our work can assist you with your research project or just allow you to recall that memorable game from the past.

We are always looking for help with our data inputting. If you would like to lend a hand just join the group and drop us a note to let us know you're interested in helping us out. We'll be happy to set you up with everthing you need to get started.
http://sports.groups.yahoo.com/group/hockey_summary_project/
You can join up and just look around if you would rather. We have lots of interesting raw data in our "Files" section. It's all free for the taking. Just give us a mention if our work helps you and your research projects. Help us out by letting the hockey fans you know about our group and our efforts.
