Society for International Hockey Research
Welcome!
We are the Society for International Hockey Research, a community of writers, statisticians, collectors, broadcasters, academics, and fans dedicated to preserving and exploring the history, culture, and impact of hockey.
Our members share a passion for uncovering the stories behind the game, from its earliest roots to the modern era, through research, discussion, and collaboration. While much of our work focuses on the past, we also examine how hockey continues to shape communities, identities, and conversations today.
 Whether documenting player careers, studying leagues long since vanished, or exploring the game's broader social and cultural role, we aim to deepen understanding and appreciation of hockey in all its dimensions.
Explore our resources, read our publications, and discover the people and moments that shaped the game and continue to influence it. If you share our interest in hockey and its place in the world, we invite you to join us.

Website Access Issues Affecting Some Members
Saturday, October 17, 2026 7:43
We have recently become aware that geographic security filtering implemented by SIHR's hosting provider may be preventing some members from accessing our website. So far, we have only confirmed reports of access problems from Slovakia.
These security measures were introduced by the hosting provider to protect its servers against malicious activity, but some of the restrictions appear to be overly aggressive, inadvertently affecting legitimate visitors. This is not the result of anything members have done. 
We are working with the provider to fine-tune these rules while maintaining appropriate security protections.
 If you are experiencing difficulty accessing the SIHR website, please let us know as soon as possible at support@sihr.ca , including your country or location. If you are aware of other members who may be having difficulty accessing the website, we would appreciate hearing from you on their behalf.
Recognizing that affected members may be unable to read this notice on our website, we will also be distributing it by email to all members.

2026 Fall Meeting
Saturday, November 7, 2026
Long-time member Kevin Slater has offered to host our Fall 2026 meeting in lovely Oakville, Ont. We are hoping to coordinate it with the 34th edition of the U17 World Challenge that will be hosted Nov. 1-7, 2026, in Oakville, at the 1,500-seat Sixteen Mile Sports Complex. So please save the weekend of Nov. 6-8 on your calendars. People
To search for people, specify a proper or common first name or nickname and a surname, or only a first or surname. For best result, specify both a first and last name.
For example, the term Gretzky will return all players with that surname, whereas the term Wayne Gretzky will only return the player with that name.
 Terms are also wild carded to the right, so the term W Gre will return Wayne Gretzky and Walter Green, among others. For finer control, go to the members-only advanced person search .
Teams and leagues
Search terms are wild carded on both ends when searching for teams and leagues, so a search for lion will return values such as Brantford Lions and 225th Battalion .
