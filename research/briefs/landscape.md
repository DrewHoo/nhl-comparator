# Brief: the league's landscape and eras (slice `landscape`)

Read `research/briefs/_shared.md` first. Report: `research/league-landscape.md`. Return: `research/landscape/return.json`. No NHL API calls (the `league-apis` agent owns that host); WebFetch/WebSearch, the helper for pages you save (budget 60), Hockey-Reference one page per lead. ## The question

What does a comparator engine built for the NFL and WNBA need to know about hockey before it counts anything? The siblings learned their sport's traps the hard way (team codes that change across files, stat-line rows vs appearances, the start flag's definition, lockout seasons, neutral sites, dates and time zones). Write the hockey list, with a receipt per claim.

## Sections

1. **Eras and stat floors.** For each stat, the first season it was officially recorded league-wide, and caveats: goals and assists (1917-18; how assists were credited early, the 1930s change to two assists); PIM; +/- (1967-68 official; earlier private tracking); shots on goal (per-game skater shots from when?); power-play and shorthanded goals; game-winning goals; TOI and shifts (1997-98? 1998-99?); faceoffs; hits, blocked shots, giveaways, takeaways (2005-06 RTSS, arena-scorer bias caveats); goalie shots against / saves (when per-game, when season-level); goalie decisions and OTL; shutouts; shootouts (2005-06); ties abolished 2005-06; OTL point 1999-2000; 3-on-3 OT 2015-16; the 1983-84 OT reintroduction; empty-net goals flagged from when. A table: stat × first season × caveat × receipt.
2. **Season lengths and interruptions.** Games per team per season from 1917-18 (22) to 82, with every change; 1918-19 final cancelled (flu); the 1994-95 (48), 2012-13 (48) lockouts and the 2004-05 cancellation; 2019-20 paused at ~70 games and the qualifying round; 2020-21 (56); any season where teams played unequal numbers of games. A table by season (or by span) with receipts.
3. **League structure and franchise identity.** NHA→NHL 1917; the Original Six era; 1967 expansion; WHA 1972-79 and the 1979 merger; and **every franchise relocation and rename** with dates (Montreal Wanderers 1917, Quebec Bulldogs→Hamilton Tigers, Ottawa→St. Louis Eagles, Pittsburgh Pirates→Philadelphia Quakers, Brooklyn Americans, Oakland/California Seals→Cleveland Barons→merged with Minnesota, Kansas City Scouts→Colorado Rockies→New Jersey Devils, Atlanta Flames→Calgary, Minnesota North Stars→Dallas, Quebec→Colorado, Winnipeg→Phoenix→Arizona→Utah, Hartford→Carolina, Atlanta Thrashers→Winnipeg, Mighty Ducks rename, and the 2026 state of Utah's name). The point: a franchise is one entity across moves in the NHL's own record book (confirm the NHL's stance: do the Jets' 1.0 records belong to Arizona/Utah? do the Thrashers' belong to Winnipeg 2.0?) and the ledger needs a franchise-id table and the team abbreviations the NHL API uses per era.
4. **What the NHL counts.** WHA stats are not NHL stats (quote the NHL; how Howe's, Hull's and Gretzky's WHA seasons are shown on NHL.com); NHA stats before 1917 (not NHL); regular season and playoffs are never combined in records; All-Star games excluded; the 1919-20 era "games played" quirks; what "games played" means for a player (dressed and played; the 2019 emergency goalie rule, David Ayres' GP and win); goalie starts vs appearances; "points" as the headline stat for skaters; wins, GAA, SV%, shutouts for goalies; plus-minus as a stat the discourse still uses for defensemen.
5. **Dates, time zones, venues.** Game dates are local; late West Coast games cross midnight UTC (the WNBA/CFB sites got dates a day off from UTC timestamps); neutral-site games (the 1992-94 neutral-site series, Global Series in Europe/Japan, the 2020 bubbles in Toronto and Edmonton, outdoor games); how home/away is assigned in the bubble; doubleheaders (none in the NHL? confirm); the NHL's game id scheme (`SSSSTTGGGG`) and the 1917-18 numbering.
6. **People and ids.** The NHL player id (8-digit, `84xxxxx`), what Hockey-Reference ids look like, Elite Prospects ids, Wikidata P3522 and P3567 (HR id?), where birthdates come from (the NHL `landing` endpoint has them; which open file has them for pre-2010 players: Hockey Databank Master table? Wikidata?), and name collisions (two "Sebastian Aho"s, both active; the Tkachuks; "Mike Smith"s). Coaches: where a coach-per-game record exists (the NHL boxscore? Hockey-Reference coach pages? Hockey Databank Coaches table).
7. **Where the legends sit.** A table of ~40 careers (first and last NHL season, WHA years if any, GP) so a floor decision can be read against it: Howe, Richard, Harvey, Béliveau, Hull, Mikita, Orr, Esposito, Lafleur, Dionne, Trottier, Bossy, Gretzky, Messier, Coffey, Bourque, Lemieux, Yzerman, Hull (Brett), Jagr, Sakic, Lidström, Selänne, Roy, Hasek, Brodeur, Thornton, Iginla, Ovechkin, Crosby, Malkin, Stamkos, Kane, MacKinnon, McDavid, Matthews, Makar, Hughes, Bedard, Celebrini, plus the goalies Sawchuk, Plante, Dryden, Fuhr, Luongo, Lundqvist, Hellebuyck. One receipt per row (one Hockey-Reference or NHL.com page per player, or Wikipedia). Then: which of them a 1917 floor, a 1967-68 floor (+/-), a 1987-88 floor, a 1997-98 floor (TOI), a 2005-06 floor, a 2007-08 floor and a 2010-11 floor would include in full, in part, or not at all.
