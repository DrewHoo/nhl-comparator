
## Part B: publisher conventions

### Answers at a glance

| Q | Answer | Strongest receipt |
|---|---|---|
| 1. "Games" | Player GP for career "fastest to" and pace claims; team games for season-start claims (50-in-50, fastest 50 or 100 from the start of a season, team fastest to N wins). Wikipedia lists player-GP 50-in-50s separately as "not an official record". | NHL.com McDavid "in 657 NHL games"; Wikipedia 50-in-50 "within their team's first 50 games" |
| 2. Regular season vs playoffs | Regular season is the default everywhere. Playoffs are separate lists. A combined figure always carries a label ("including playoffs", "professional"). | Wikipedia records list; QuantHockey tabs; Guinness "regular seasons" |
| 3. WHA | Excluded from NHL records. H-R separates the tables, Wikipedia mentions combined totals only as an aside, and NHL.com's "professional" goals are NHL + WHA + playoffs. | Wikipedia 1979 expansion: the NHL "did not and does not recognize the WHA's records" |
| 4. Age | NHL.com: years + days on the game date. "Teenager" = before the 20th birthday. Wikipedia's goalie milestones use years + months; Guinness uses years + months. | NHL.com "(18 years, 253 days)"; Laine "before my 20th birthday" |
| 5. Appearance | Any appearance is a GP (a 7.6-second goalie career is a career). Goalie records count appearances. NHL.com separates starts from games when it matters. An emergency backup gets a game and a win. | Jorge Alves 7.6 sec; NHL.com "47 games (46 starts)"; Ayres |
| 6. Season bound | Team games from opening night. The list carries Gretzky 39 and Lemieux team game 46 (his 44th GP). Gretzky 42 is UNVERIFIED (records.nhl.com not readable). | Wikipedia records and 50-in-50 page |
| 7. Early assists | Caveats exist but come from AP (NHL's own correction project) and analysts, not from record pages. AP/CP sometimes floor at 1943-44; NHL.com does not. | SI/AP "up to four assists"; DFO "1945-46"; TSN "since 1943-44" |
| 8. Ties, OTL, SO | Team "losses" = regulation losses in NHL standings. The four streak types are distinct. A shootout win is a goalie win (Brodeur's 48 include 10, derived). A 0-0 shootout gives both goalies a shutout. Points % = (2W+T+OTL)/2GP. | ESPN definition; Wikipedia team-record notes; statistical-leaders notes |
| 9. "Through N games" | The player's own GP. Missed team games are not in N. | NHL.com "659 games"; TSN "602nd game"; Wikipedia "Game No." column |

### Five conventions that carry the most weight

1. **Career milestones use player GP, season-start records use team games.** The board needs both windows, and the "50 in 50" family must use the team's games or it will rank unofficial seasons as official.
2. **Regular season only, playoffs never summed.** Playoffs get their own board.
3. **NHL only.** WHA is never folded in. Howe, Hull and Gretzky boards are NHL-only, with a Method note.
4. **NHL standings only count regulation defeats as losses.** Team futility streaks must say "winless" or "losing" and name the era. ESPN and the NHL disagree in public.
5. **Ages are given as years + days on the game date, and "teenager" means before the 20th birthday.** This needs DOB data, which the 2010+ release lacks.
