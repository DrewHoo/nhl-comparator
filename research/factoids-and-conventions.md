## Summary (10 lines)

1. **Scope.** 64 of 70 claims are regular season only. The 4 playoff claims sit on separate lists, and no publisher sums regular season and playoffs except under an explicit label ("including playoffs", or NHL.com's "professional" for NHL + WHA). **Default: regular season, NHL only.**
2. **Unit.** Career "fastest to N", "through N games" and pace claims count the **player's own GP**: McDavid 1,000 points in 659, Makar 100 points in 108, Lundqvist 400 wins in 727. Season-start claims count **team games**: 50-in-50, fastest 50 or 100 from the start of a season, Jets fastest to 15 wins.
3. Lemieux 1988-89 has both units in one sentence: team game 46, his own 44th game. The official list uses team games and the popular "50 in 44" uses his GP. The comparator needs both windows and has to label which one it uses.
4. **WHA.** The NHL does not recognize WHA records. Hockey-Reference prints NHL and WHA as separate tables (Howe 1,850 NHL, 508 WHA). Wikipedia mentions combined totals only as an aside. NHL.com combines them only as "professional" goals. **No WHA on the board.**
5. **Default board.** Careers, regular season, NHL only, x = the player's own GP (goalie appearances for goalies, games coached for coaches, team games for team-seasons). Add a team-game window for season-start claims.
6. The sentence needs a **"by age" window** (youngest/oldest to M, "as a teenager" = before the 20th birthday; NHL.com gives years + days) and a **streak agg** (point streaks, winning/unbeaten/winless/losing streaks).
7. **Team results words differ by era.** NHL standings call only regulation defeats losses. "Winless", "losing", "unbeaten" and "points streak" are four different streaks. Point totals before and after 1983, 1999 and 2005 are not strictly comparable, so team futility needs those era words.
8. **Era bounds publishers already print:** "salary cap era" (Matthews 69), "since 1995-96" (McDavid 153), "since 1943-44" (AP/CP on Makar), "shootout era" (ESPN on the Sabres), "with one point awarded for an overtime or shootout loss" (Sharks 47 points).
9. **Early assists are soft.** The NHL fixed 6,000+ items for 1917-87, and some years awarded up to four assists per goal. Secondary assists were not full-time until 1945-46. AP/CP floors some lists at 1943-44, while NHL.com does not.
10. **Corpus.** 70 claims: 68 [V], 2 UNVERIFIED with no publisher list ("wins through first 100 starts", "fewest wins through N"). Five corrections to the seed list are under Rejections.

### Five conventions that carry the most weight

1. **Career milestones use player GP, season-start records use team games.** The board needs both windows, and the "50 in 50" family must use the team's games or it will rank unofficial seasons as official.
2. **Regular season only, playoffs never summed.** Playoffs get their own board.
3. **NHL only.** WHA is never folded in. Howe, Hull and Gretzky boards are NHL-only, with a Method note.
4. **NHL standings only count regulation defeats as losses.** Team futility streaks must say "winless" or "losing" and name the era. ESPN and the NHL disagree in public.
5. **Ages are given as years + days on the game date, and "teenager" means before the 20th birthday.** This needs DOB data, which the 2010+ release lacks.

### Q3 WHA

- **Hockey-Reference**: Leader pages print NHL and WHA as separate tables under one "NHL & WHA" title; Howe is 1,850 in the NHL table and 508 in the WHA table, never summed. - **Wikipedia (List of NHL records; 300-win list)**: NHL-only; WHA totals appear only as a combined aside. ### Q7 early assists

[V] “Six years of poring over scoresheets and summaries of games between 1917 and 1987 by an NHL statistics team has found and fixed more than 6,000 bits of information that were overlooked or miscounted in the league's early eras.” ([src](https://www.si.com/nhl/2017/09/26/ap-hkn-canadiens-rocket-assist)) · “There were years when up to four assists were awarded on a goal.” ([src](https://www.si.com/nhl/2017/09/26/ap-hkn-canadiens-rocket-assist))

[V] “Assists were also originally awarded only on passes within the offensive zone, and, after some on and off experimenting, secondary assists weren’t handed out full-time until the 1945-46 season onward.” ([src](https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/news/the-10-greatest-cy-young-seasons-in-nhl-history))

### Q8 ties/OTL/SO

- **NHL standings / ESPN**: NHL standings count only regulation defeats as losses, so an OT/SO run is a "winless streak"; ESPN pushes back and counts all defeats. [V] “because the NHL only considers it a loss when the defeat occurs in regulation” ([src](https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/31166656/the-buffalo-sabres-epic-winless-streak-continues-most-fault-how-do-get-back-track)) · “I know this is a real wacky concept, but a loss is a loss, and the Sabres have 18 of them in a row.” ([src](https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/31166656/the-buffalo-sabres-epic-winless-streak-continues-most-fault-how-do-get-back-track))
- **Wikipedia (List of NHL records (team))**: Streak definitions: undefeated = wins + ties; winless = regulation, OT, SO losses and ties; losing = regulation losses (plus OT losses 1983-99). Point totals are not comparable across 1983, 1999 and 2005. [V] “A winless streak includes losses in regulation, overtime, shootouts, or ties.” ([src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NHL_records_(team))) · “These changes in points awarded therefore make strict comparisons in wins, losses, and ties (after overtime, 1983–99, and in regulation, 1999–present) before and after these dates slightly problematic.” ([src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NHL_records_(team)))
- **Wikipedia (List of NHL statistical leaders / head coaches)**: Points percentage = (2W + T + OTL) / 2GP; a 0-0 game decided by shootout gives both goalies a shutout; a shootout win counts as a goalie win (Brodeur's 48 included 10 SO wins). [V] “Points percentage is determined by the number of points a team earns (equal to the number of ties and overtime losses, plus twice the number of wins) divided by the total possible points (equal to twice the number of games).” ([src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NHL_statistical_leaders)) · “Since 2005-06, if the game ends in a shootout after a 0-0 scoreless tie, both teams' goaltenders are credited with a shutout, regardless of how many shots are conceded during the shootout.” ([src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NHL_statistical_leaders)) · “Most ties, career: Terry Sawchuk, 172” ([src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NHL_records_(individual)))
- **ESPN Stats & Info**: ESPN bounds a winless record at the shootout era when the definition changed. [V] “They own the record for the longest winless streak of the shootout era (since 2005-06)” ([src](https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/31166656/the-buffalo-sabres-epic-winless-streak-continues-most-fault-how-do-get-back-track))
